## Choreography vs Orchestration

### This Implementation: Choreography ?

```
SenderApp           RabbitMQ           ClientA
    ?                  ?                  ?
    ?  Publish Event   ?                  ?
    ????????????????????                  ?
    ?                  ?  Deliver Event   ?
    ?                  ????????????????????
    ?                  ?                  ?  Process
    ?                  ?                  ?  Independently
    ?                  ?                  ?
    
? No direct coupling
? SenderApp doesn't know about ClientA
? Easy to add ClientB, ClientC, etc.
? Each service makes its own decisions
```

### Alternative: Orchestration (Not Implemented)

```
SenderApp       Orchestrator        ClientA
    ?               ?                  ?
    ?  Request      ?                  ?
    ?????????????????                  ?
    ?               ?  Command         ?
    ?               ????????????????????

    ?               ????????????????????

    ?????????????????                  ?
    
? Central coordinator
? Orchestrator knows about all services
? More complex to maintain
```
